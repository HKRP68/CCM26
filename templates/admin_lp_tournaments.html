How it works

A Lets Play Tournament is the Challenge League tournament with people instead of franchises: every entrant plays 20 overs with their own roster, cards and traits, and every result feeds a points table with net run rate, per-player leaderboards and an optional playoff bracket. One Lets Play tournament and one Challenge League tournament can be live at once.

  1. Create the tournament below.
  2. On Players & setup, add each entrant by their Telegram id.
  3. Generate the schedule (or skip it for free-play), then press Start.
  4. Players reply to their opponent with /lptour to play a fixture, and follow the competition with /lpt, /lptable, /lptfixtures and /lptstats. Admins can do all of the above from chat too — see /lptadmin.

Create a tournament

Every Lets Play match is 20 overs, so the format is fixed. The new tournament becomes the active one but stays in draft until you start it.
