This page manages the server-side version of cricket_card_generator_website_v7-1.html. Upload a blank card template for each rarity tab, then tune the shared font, cutout, flag, and text layout. Every card reuses the cutout uploaded on that player's edit page. Base, Star, and Legend templates are independent; if the matching template is missing, the normal card priority continues to the original auto-generated card.

🎨 Automatic card priority

Cards are selected automatically in this order: custom full card → website card with player image → original auto-generated card.
