🚫 Blocked Rating Ranges

A blocked range is a hard veto: those ratings are never handed out by the sources you tick, no matter what the rarity table says. Use it to retire a rating band without rewriting your tiers — untick the rule later and the ratings come straight back. Claim covers /claim, /daily and the Mystery Box's rarity pulls, Drop covers packs and free packs, and Lucky Card covers the wheel and its Mini App twin. Buying, trading, the market and admin grants are never filtered — those are deliberate, not random.
