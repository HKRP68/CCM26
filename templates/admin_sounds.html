🔊 Gameplay Sound Management

Sounds played inside the Crickidex Arena Mini App (/wpm and /wpmbot matches). Each key has one active sound: the built-in default that ships with the app, or your uploaded replacement. In-Game sounds fire on ball outcomes; Match Ambience alternates as crowd background while an innings is live. Players can mute either group from the game header.

💡 Telegram-channel storage (recommended for uploads): with MEDIA_STORAGE_CHAT_ID configured (same channel as Event Media), uploaded audio is stored on Telegram forever and survives every redeploy. Without it, uploads go to local disk and may reset on deploy. Defaults always survive — they are part of the app itself.
