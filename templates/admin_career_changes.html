✏️ Career name & country changes

🎖 Career players 🔤 Name pool

Every owner gets one free change of their Career Player's name and country. After that they pay on the ladder below — and only while paid changes are open. A name rolled from the curated pool is already vetted and applies itself; a name somebody typed lands in the queue here, and their card keeps its current name until you approve it. Gems are taken when the request is made and refunded in full when you reject it — a refused name costs the owner nothing and doesn't use up a change.
