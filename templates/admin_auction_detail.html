Settings

Anti-snipe. A bid arriving with the window or less on the clock pushes the deadline out to the extend value, at most max times per lot. Setting the window equal to the extension (10 / 10 / 5) gives the rule most rooms actually want — any bid in the final ten seconds gives everybody ten more. At a two-second window, a bid with three seconds left buys nobody a chance to answer.

Min squad drives the reachability rule: a franchise may never bid so much that it could not still fill its remaining slots at the pool's cheapest base price ({{ money(season.min_base_price_lakh) }}). Every franchise's ceiling is shown below and on the board, because a rule you only meet once you have broken it is one you cannot plan around.

🪪 Right To Match {% if season.rtm_enabled %}on{% else %}off{% endif %}

The IPL 2025 rule, in full. When a lot's clock expires, the franchise that held the player last season is asked whether it wants to use an RTM. If it says yes, the standing top bidder gets one more raise — and only then does the holder match that final number, or let him go. The price is the final bid, not the one that triggered it.
Each of the three windows times out to the safe answer, so a franchise nobody is running can never wedge the auction.

A premium of 0 is the pure IPL rule — matching costs exactly the final bid. Set it higher and holding a card costs something to use. Saving deals the card count out to every franchise that has not spent one yet; a franchise mid-auction keeps whatever it has left.

🆕 Expansion picks

A side joining a league that already exists has nobody to retain, so it would walk into the auction empty-handed against everyone else's kept players. Picks let it sign a few first, out of the pool nobody retained — the rule the IPL used for Gujarat and Lucknow in 2022.

Who counts as "new" is worked out, not ticked: a side is new when last season's league records nobody as theirs. So this depends on the auction following the right league — set that below. Saving deals the picks out; a side that has already used one keeps what it has left. A pick costs the retention ladder's rung for that side's next pick, and can be overridden per pick.
