# Tournament records and presentations

- Records stay silent during play. Once the scorecard is committed, the group receives one `match-<id>-records.md` document containing the match recap and every broken or equalled season/all-time record. Corrected scorecards update that document. Existing live-watch notifications are no longer delivered.
- `/seasons record [competition]` downloads the latest season's current holders, including joint holders and rate qualification minimums. The season and record cards also have a Records file button and an opener-only Close button.
- League fixtures and each playoff stage appear in separate sections in Telegram, the tournament Mini App and the admin overview.
- League fixtures repeat **four Night, two Day** in fixture order; playoffs are always Night. The period drives actual weather/dew conditions. Free-play matches keep their existing random conditions.
- **Tournament → Manage → Playoff pitches and stadiums** sets neutral playoff conditions in advance. Empty selections inherit the tournament defaults. Changes apply to unstarted playoffs, leaving played/live fixtures alone.
- **Tournament → Schedule → Match finish** lets website admins choose normal play, one Super Over or two Super Overs for an unstarted fixture. This control is never included in public payloads or announcements. A broad chase-pacing corridor keeps a competitive run requirement through the final over, leaving scoring runs for the final legal ball instead of freezing the score early. Natural dots, singles, boundaries, extras and nonterminal wickets are retained when they fit that corridor. Scoring is steered before deliveries are recorded, so the scorecard and result agree. A two-Super-Over setting ties the first Super Over before opening the second; subsequent unresolved ties retain the normal replay rules. The feature applies to matches played through Challenge League and Lets Play, including their Arena play flows. Manual result entry and deadline simulations retain their explicit result controls.

## Awards ceremony

Prizes continue to pay once when the final is decided. Presentations wait for an admin to run `/awardceremony`, or `/awardceremony #<tournament-id>` when choosing a finished tournament. Each award is presented in a separate photo message, two seconds apart, with the winner, team, contribution and actual prize. Uploaded team logos are sent as image bytes. If Telegram rejects a photo URL, the ceremony tries an uploaded award card; if artwork cannot be rendered or accepted, it presents the full award text instead. Temporary delivery failures leave the award pending and show the exact `/awardceremony #<tournament-id>` command to resume, skipping awards already sent and never paying prizes again.

Set team ceremony photos on **Tournament → Manage → participating team → Save team photo** using an HTTPS image URL. Without one, the team's logo/crest is used. Players use their generated player cards with their team and tournament contribution; imported players without master artwork get a named award card.

The ceremony presents only these categories, in order: **Orange Cap, Purple Cap, Most Sixes, Most Fours, Most Valuable Player, Player of the Final, Emerging Player, Highest Strike Rate, Most Catches, Most Dot Balls, Winning Captain**. Champion/runner-up honours remain in historical records rather than being extra ceremony posts.

Highest Strike Rate uses the tournament's minimum batting balls. Most Dot Balls counts recorded bowling dots, falling back to verified delivery evidence for older scorecards. Player of the Final uses the saved match Player of the Match selection. Winning Captain is the actual user who captained the winning side in the final, falling back to the team's owner for manual/simulated fixtures. Most Catches is automatic: total verified catches across completed matches, including keeper catches and caught-and-bowled, excluding stumpings and run-outs. Equal totals are resolved by player name, then team name. An award without evidence is explicitly marked as unawarded; performances are not fabricated.

Prizes for each category are configurable on Manage. Command aliases include `/tprize sixes`, `fours`, `final`, `sr`, `catches`, `dots`, and `captain`, followed by coins and optional gems. A previously configured catch prize carries into Most Catches. Newly added categories are backfilled for finished tournaments when their ceremony is opened, without repaying existing awards.

## Channel results and ceremony posts

Configure multiple destinations in **Tournament → Manage → Match results and ceremony channels**, one `@channelusername` or `-100…` channel ID per line. The admin command `/tchannels [#tournament-id] add @channel1 -1001234567890` does the same, including private channels by ID; use `remove`, `clear`, or the bare command to inspect them. The bot must be an administrator with permission to post in every destination.

Every completed match posts its summary scorecard **with the text as its caption**. The caption includes the competition, stage, scores in cricket overs, result, Player of the Match and contributions, and a champions line for a decided final. Played matches include **Tap to view full match** and a Mini App link for that exact match, using the existing `BOT_USERNAME` and `MINIAPP_NAME` configuration. This link remains in the caption even when an exceptionally long summary moves onto the image. Super Over matches are one grouped media post containing the main summary and the Super Over scorecard; the caption lists every recorded Super Over's innings and the actual deciding margin, including countback when used. The initial tied main innings is not published as a final result before its Super Over ends. Manual and deadline-simulated results also queue scorecard posts after commit; a fixture without an actual match has no full-match link.

Each ceremony photo/caption is mirrored to those channels. Failed destinations retry independently, while successful destinations are skipped. A 30-second delivery worker recovers pending posts, and Manage displays recent delivery errors and a retry button. Adding a channel affects new match and ceremony events; it does not flood the channel with old results. Channels receive image/caption posts, including a generated fallback card if artwork is unavailable. Exceptionally long summaries are included on the image to respect Telegram's caption length limit.

**Emerging Player rule:** highest tournament MVP points among players rated **83 or below**, with at least half the appearances of the most-used player (rounded up). Lower rating breaks equal MVP points. Players with unknown ratings are ineligible. This rewards performance from lower-rated players without imposing IPL age rules on the game's roster.
